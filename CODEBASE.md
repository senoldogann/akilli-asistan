## Notes

- `scan_results.json` is a repo-level verification artifact referenced by dependency audit scripts.
- `ZeroLose/` contains the product code, tests, packaging output, and app-specific documentation.
- Provider composition lives in `opencode.json` (`opencode-go/`, `opencode/`, `deepseek/`)
  and is regenerated by `scripts/sync_agents.py`. DeepSeek models use `reasoning: true`
  with `interleaved.reasoning_content` so the app can render a collapsible thinking panel.
- ZeroLose streams model reasoning traces into `ChatMessage.thinking` (DeepSeek/OpenAI
  `reasoning_content`) and displays them via `ThinkingCollapsePanel` (collapsed by default).
- Provider-specific agent adapters must remain thin and defer shared policy to `AGENTS.md`, `.agent/SYSTEM.md`, and `.agent/rules/`.
